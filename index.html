Questions

The things people ask before they call.

How many clients do you take on each year?
I work with a small number of clients at a time — so every client gets a senior partner, not a junior. You get direct access to me across strategy, design, copy and production, plus a strong network of specialists I bring in for the right parts of the job.
What areas of Melbourne do you work with?
I'm based in South East Melbourne and primarily work with local businesses across the Bayside corridor — Chelsea, Bonbeach, Mordialloc, Seaford, Edithvale, Aspendale — and broader Greater Melbourne.
How is a branding and website project priced?
Every project is scoped individually — branding, website and local SEO can be combined into one fixed-price package built around what you need. Fixed pricing — no timesheet surprises.
How long does a project take?
Most projects go from first call to a live brand and website in 2–6 weeks.
Do you do video and content as well as branding?
Yes — I've led creative across campaigns and content for brands including Volvo, Australian Defence Force and Nando's. Video and content is where I'm genuinely different to most branding studios. Brand films, testimonials and social cut-downs made to sell, not just to look good.
