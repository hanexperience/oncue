1 Strategy

  • Find out what people actually type into Google — "demolition near me", "house demolition cost", "site clearing", and the like
  • Look at what competitors rank for, and where the gaps are we can win
  • Map the website around your services — residential, commercial, excavation, site clearing, and anything else you do
  • Set clear goals — enquiries per month, and how we'll track them

2 Website Redesign

  • Rebuild the site so it looks sharp, loads fast, and works properly on a phone
  • Make it dead simple to get a quote — clear "call" and "request a quote" buttons on every page
  • Rewrite the pages so it's obvious what you do and why to choose Dig Dig
  • Set up a proper gallery for before-and-after shots of your jobs

3 Search & Visibility

  • Build the search terms into the site so Google knows what you do and where
  • Set up and fully optimise the Google Business Profile — the biggest single lever for a local trade
  • Make sure the site is indexed and set up to rank in your service area (SEO)
  • Create the YouTube channel and set it up ready for uploads

4 Content Foundation

  • Set up the blog to answer the questions people ask you over and over
  • Structure it so every post can link to a video and back to your services
  • Put a system in place to capture footage and photos from every job going forward
  • Keep the tone straightforward — no jargon, the way you'd explain it on site
