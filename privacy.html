Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Beets & Co ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of everyone who visits beetsandco.com.au or engages us for branding, web, marketing, video or AI automation services. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can contact us about it.

1. Who we are

Beets & Co is a founder-led branding and marketing agency operating out of Bayside, Melbourne, run by Michael Beets. For any privacy enquiry, contact michael@beetsandco.com.au or call +61 447 542 930.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us directly — your name, email address, phone number, business name and any project details you share through our enquiry form, email or phone when you get in touch about a project.
  • Website usage data — pages visited, referral source, device and browser type, approximate location and general interaction data, collected automatically through analytics tools.
  • Client project data — where we're engaged for a project, information relevant to delivering that work (e.g. brand assets, business details, access to accounts you ask us to manage).

3. Cookies & analytics

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it over time. We currently use:

  • Google Analytics — to measure site traffic and behaviour in aggregate.
  • PostHog — a product analytics tool used to understand how visitors navigate the site, so we can improve content and usability.

These tools may set cookies or use similar identifiers in your browser. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time — the site will still function, though some analytics data may be lost.

4. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss potential projects.
  • To deliver, manage and communicate about work we've been engaged to do.
  • To understand and improve how our website performs.
  • To meet legal, accounting or administrative obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we don't use your details for purposes beyond those described here without asking first.

5. Sharing your information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us run the business and deliver client work — for example hosting providers, analytics platforms (Google, PostHog), email and automation tools, and specialist contractors brought in for a specific project. These providers only receive the information needed to do their job and are expected to handle it securely.

6. Data storage & security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. Data is stored with reputable third-party providers (e.g. hosting, email and analytics platforms) that maintain their own security standards. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to keep risk as low as practical.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to ask what we're using it for. To make a request, email michael@beetsandco.com.au — we'll respond as promptly as we can.

8. Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites (client work, social profiles, tools we recommend). We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites — check their own policies before sharing information with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our tools or practices change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent version.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how your information is handled? Reach out at michael@beetsandco.com.au or +61 447 542 930.
