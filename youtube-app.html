Beets & Co — YouTube App

This page describes the "Beets & Co" application registered with Google's OAuth platform for use with the YouTube Data API.

Beets & Co is a marketing agency working with local trades, builders, and professional services clients. This application is an internal tool used by Beets & Co staff to upload and manage video content on YouTube channels on behalf of our marketing clients — including publishing videos, updating video metadata, and reviewing channel content and basic performance data.

Access is limited to Beets & Co team members managing client YouTube channels. The application does not share, sell, or use this data for any purpose beyond the video publishing and management services described above.

For details on what data is accessed and how it is handled, see our Privacy Policy.
