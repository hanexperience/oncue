The nine doors

Every one of these is a way in. Or a way past you.

This isn't a framework I invented for a landing page. It's the checklist I run for clients every week — the same nine columns, tracked job by job. I've just made it public so you can score yourself against it.

01

The Search Door

You come up when someone types what you do plus their suburb. Not your business name — the job.

02

The Map Door

Your Google Business Profile: the box with the map, the photos and the reviews. Complete, and actually alive.

03

The Word-of-Mouth Door

Recent Google reviews — and replies underneath them. The only thing on this list a stranger trusts more than your own website.

04

The Listings Door

Your name, address and phone number, matching everywhere they appear. Old numbers cost jobs quietly.

05

The Feed Door

Instagram and Facebook showing recent, finished work. Proof you're busy, without having to say it.

06

The Watch Door

Video. Where people go to research before they commit to something expensive — and where almost no trade shows up.

07

The Room Door

The conversations you're not in. Local Facebook groups and Reddit threads where someone asks for a recommendation.

08

The Press Door

Being mentioned by people who already have trust — local papers, industry sites, other people's blogs.

09

The Answer Door

What happens after they message you. The one nearly everybody skips, and the cheapest one to fix.

+

The AI Door

Whether ChatGPT and Google's AI name you when someone asks them for a recommendation. Not scored — but you'll get a read on it at the end.

Three minutes and you'll know which of the nine are shut.
