SEO for Melbourne electricians.

EV charger installations, switchboard upgrades, solar connections — high-value work that homeowners search for right now. Here's how to rank for it in your Melbourne suburbs.

M
Michael Beets
Founder & Director, Beets & Co

Melbourne electrical is becoming a high-value SEO landscape. EV charger installations are growing fast across the inner-city and Bayside suburbs. Switchboard upgrades are a consistent $1,500–$4,000 job with high search volume. Solar connections and battery storage are pushing homeowners to search for licensed electricians who know the work.

Most Melbourne electricians are still relying on word of mouth and occasionally Hipages. SEO competition in electrical is thinner than plumbing — which means there's a real window to own your suburbs before that changes.

Melbourne electricians with suburb-specific pages for EV charger and switchboard work are finding near-zero competition in the local pack — for now.

The four ranking drivers

01

Google Business Profile

Set your primary category to "Electrician." Add secondary categories: "Electric Vehicle Charging Station Contractor", "Solar Energy Contractor" if relevant. List every Melbourne suburb you serve in your service area. Upload job photos regularly — EV charger installs, switchboards, outdoor wiring. Post fortnightly.

02

Reviews

Most Melbourne electricians have 5–15 reviews. Get to 40+ and you'll be in the top tier for your service area. The 3× click rate advantage for businesses with 40+ reviews applies just as much to electrical as any other trade. Build a text-message review request into your job completion process now.

03

On-page SEO and suburb pages

Build dedicated pages for your highest-value services in your key suburbs. "EV Charger Installation Cheltenham," "Switchboard Upgrade Mentone," "Solar Electrician Parkdale." Each page needs a clear title tag, an H1 with the service and suburb, a paragraph of genuine local content, and a schema-marked service offering.

04

Suburbs to target first

For Bayside: Cheltenham, Mentone, Parkdale, Mordialloc, Cheltenham, Sandringham, Brighton. For South-East: Clayton, Oakleigh, Bentleigh, Carnegie, Caulfield. For inner-east: Hawthorn, Camberwell, Balwyn. Prioritise suburbs with high home ownership and renovation activity — the EV charger and switchboard market follows property value.

SEO checklist for Melbourne electricians

✓GBP category set to "Electrician" with relevant secondary categories
✓All Melbourne service suburbs listed in GBP service area
✓20+ real job photos uploaded to GBP
✓Review collection system in place — aiming for 2–3 per month
✓Website has suburb-specific pages for highest-value services
✓ElectricalContractor schema markup on homepage
✓NAP consistent across website, GBP, Yellow Pages, True Local
✓Website loads in under 3 seconds on mobile

SEO vs Google Ads: what to prioritise

Google Ads for electrical keywords in Melbourne can cost $20–50 per click in competitive areas. SEO clicks cost nothing once you've earned the ranking. The right approach for most Melbourne electricians is to start SEO now and use ads to fill the gap during the first 3–6 months while SEO builds.

For EV charger and solar keywords specifically, the SEO competition is still low enough that with the right pages and a solid GBP, organic rankings can arrive faster than the typical 6-month window. These are searches worth targeting now.
