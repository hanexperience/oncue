▸  A short filter

What to look for in an agency.

If you're evaluating digital marketing agencies as a Frankston business, run them through these four questions before you sign anything.

Do they understand your industry?A generalist that also does e-commerce and hospitality won't think like a trade business. You want someone who knows your customer — a homeowner or site manager deciding under time pressure, on a phone.
Do they give you fixed pricing?Hourly rates and open-ended retainers protect the agency's margin, not yours. Fixed project pricing and clear retainer scopes let you budget properly.
Can they show local results?Not case studies from Sydney. Melbourne rankings, Melbourne clients, Melbourne trade businesses. Local search is local — the agency should know the geography.
Calls and jobs, or clicks and impressions?If every conversation is about traffic metrics and your phone isn't ringing more, something is wrong.
