Questions

The things people ask before they call.

What does an AI chatbot actually do for a trade business?

It answers your website visitors instantly — day or night — using your real pricing, services and FAQs. It asks the right qualifying questions (job type, suburb, urgency), captures contact details, and either books a time or hands the hot lead straight to you. It's the difference between a missed after-hours enquiry and a booked job.

Is this just a generic chatbot plugin?

No. I build and train it specifically on your business — your services, your pricing structure, your service areas and your tone of voice. It's wired into your website and your lead flow, not a bolt-on widget with generic scripted answers.

Will an AI chatbot replace me answering the phone?

No — it handles the moments you can't: after hours, mid-job, or when three people enquire at once. It qualifies and captures the lead so you're calling back a warm, ready customer instead of chasing a cold missed call the next morning.

What else can be automated besides chat?

Review requests sent automatically after a job is marked complete, lead follow-up sequences for quotes that have gone quiet, and simple booking confirmations. All of it runs in the background without you touching a spreadsheet.

How is AI automation priced?

Every project at Beets & Co is scoped individually. AI automation is usually built alongside a website or as an add-on to an existing site. Fixed pricing — you'll know exactly what's included before committing.
