06
▸ A worked example

The knockdown rebuild play.

This is what "content with a purpose" looks like in practice — one search opportunity, followed all the way through.

STEP 1

We found the demand.

"knockdown rebuild" — 720/mo. "knockdown rebuild melbourne" — 480/mo. "knockdown rebuild cost melbourne" — 50/mo, $4.54 CPC.

STEP 2

We shot for it.

Filmed Tim on an active knockdown rebuild site, talking through cost, process and what surprises people.

STEP 3

We fanned it out.

Long-form blog post, YouTube upload, three vertical cut-downs, Google Business posts, Medium article.

STEP 4

It ranked.

"knockdown rebuild cost melbourne" — 18 → #1 in 6 days. "knockdown rebuild" (720/mo) — new, entered at position 15.

"We didn't shoot a video and hope. We found where the market was already looking, then made something worth finding."
