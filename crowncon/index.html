▸  How we work

Every client tells their story
a different way.

There's no template for this. Marketing for a home builder isn't one-size-fits-all — it's about learning who the client actually is, then finding the way of telling their story that makes sense for them. With Crowncon, that meant building around Tim.

Tim's a natural in front of a camera.

Some clients want to stay behind the scenes. Tim isn't one of them — he's comfortable on camera, and he tells a story well. So that's the approach we built: Tim as the face of Crowncon, on-site, talking through the build in his own words.

That's not a formula we arrived at on day one. It's evolved as the relationship has strengthened — the more we understand how Tim works and what he's proud of, the more precise the content gets. Trust builds the story as much as strategy does.

And the arrangement takes real weight off him. We run the marketing end-to-end — strategy, content, video, SEO. We build, he approves. That frees Tim to focus on what he does best: building.

Why this matters

We don't walk in with a fixed playbook. Every client gets asked the same starting question: how do you tell your story?

For Tim, that's camera-first. For another client it might be written, or product-led, or entirely behind the scenes. The channel changes. The job doesn't — put the client first, and build the story around them.

▸  Presence & trust

Being everywhere the
right people look.

Trust isn't built on one channel. We've taken Crowncon beyond the website and put them where their audience actually spends time — with a consistent voice on every platform, and someone watching what's said about them everywhere else.

Built out accounts

TikTok, YouTube, Reddit and Medium — each one active, each one carrying Crowncon's voice in a format that suits it.

Watching, not just posting

We monitor Reddit and Facebook for the conversations that matter — where trust is won or lost before someone ever reaches the site.

Directory presence

Listed and kept current across the directories that matter for a Melbourne builder — another signal of legitimacy, another way to be found.

None of this is a one-off setup. It's an ongoing strategy — new platforms as they matter, consistent activity on the ones that already work, and eyes on where the conversation moves next.

▸  Built for the client

We didn't just market the business —
we built Tim a tool for it.

Beyond content and campaigns, we built Crowncon a feasibility report tool — something Tim can put in front of a prospective client early, to help them understand what's actually possible on their block before either side commits time to it. It's a small thing that makes the whole sales conversation easier for him.

Why we build tools, not just content

A good marketing partner doesn't stop at what shows up on Instagram. If a tool would make a client's day-to-day easier — closing deals faster, answering the same question fewer times — we'll build it. It's still their story. We're just making it easier to tell.
