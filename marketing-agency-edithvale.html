FAQ

Common questions.

Can my trade business actually rank in Edithvale?

Yes — and faster than you might expect. Edithvale has very low competition for most trade categories in Google. A well-built site with a suburb-specific page and a properly set up Google Business Profile can rank in the local results within 30–90 days.

How is pricing structured?

Every project at Beets & Co is scoped individually — branding, website and local SEO combined into one package, sized to your business. All pricing is fixed — you'll know exactly what's included before you commit to anything.

Do I need a separate page for each suburb I work in?

Yes. Google ranks individual pages, not websites. A dedicated page for "electrician Edithvale" — with real local content, structured data, and internal links — is how you show Google you serve this suburb specifically. I build these pages as part of every project.

What kind of trade businesses do you work with?

Plumbers, electricians, builders, landscapers, HVAC, tilers, concreters, painters and similar trades. If you work in Edithvale and the surrounding Bayside suburbs, I understand your customer and how they search.
