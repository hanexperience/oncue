02
The client

A demolition crew that treats every site like its own.

Dig Dig Demolition is a family-owned contractor out of Carrum Downs. House demolition, licensed Class B asbestos removal and site clearing across Melbourne's South East and the Mornington Peninsula, for builders and homeowners clearing a block.

The work was already good. The reviews say so. What they needed was a steady presence everywhere a builder or homeowner looks before they pick up the phone.

03
The package

One studio running
the whole of it.

Same model as Crowncon: I'm the senior partner on every part. No handoffs between a social person, an SEO person and a video person.

Socials

Regular posting built from real jobs: machines moving, sites cleared, handovers. Demolition is naturally watchable, so the content doesn't need dressing up.

Local SEO

Every page has its own job. One page owns the broad "demolition contractors, South East Melbourne" search, a separate page owns "house demolition", and asbestos has its own. Schema, suburb coverage and a visible FAQ back it up.

Video content

Filmed on site, not in a studio. Demolition footage cut for the channels where people actually watch it, and the same footage reused across the website.

Why demolition is a good fit

A cleared site is the most honest before-and-after in any trade. Dig Dig's customers say the same thing in their reviews: organised, tidy, finished on schedule, and a pool and a slab left standing when they were meant to be.

My job is to put that in front of the people searching for it, and keep it there month after month.
