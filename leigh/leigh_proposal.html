1 Strategy

  • Research what local competitors are doing online — so we know what works and where the gaps are
  • Map out the website: services, gallery, about, contact — built around how homeowners actually shop for a builder
  • Make sure the site loads fast and works well on mobile, where most local searches happen
  • Set clear goals — enquiries per month, and how we'll track them

2 Brand Basics

  • Establish colours, fonts, and a simple visual style that feels trustworthy and local
  • Create a short set of brand guidelines so the site, socials, and any signage match
  • Define the tone — straightforward, no-nonsense, the way word-of-mouth referrals already talk about the work
  • Set up a structure for collecting before-and-after photos from every job going forward

3 Search & Visibility

  • Identify what homeowners actually type into Google when they need an extension or renovation done
  • Set up and fully optimise the Google Business Profile — the single biggest lever for a local trade business
  • Make sure the site itself is indexed and set up to rank in the local area (SEO)

4 Messaging

  • Turn the story behind the business — reputation, experience, word-of-mouth trust — into website copy
  • Make it clear why a homeowner should call this business over a competitor
  • Plan what goes on each page so the journey from "just looking" to "enquiry" makes sense
  • Keep the tone consistent everywhere the business shows up online
