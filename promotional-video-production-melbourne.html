(02)  Why promotional video works

What a promo video actually does.

Not views, not impressions. Conversion — the decision by a hesitant customer to make contact rather than leave.

It builds trust before a word is read

A 90-second promo video communicates more about who you are and how you work than five pages of copy. For businesses where trust is the primary buying decision — trades, services, professional services — video shortens that trust-building process significantly.

It keeps people on the page

A visitor who watches a video stays on your website significantly longer than one who doesn't. That increased dwell time signals quality to Google and gives you more time to make the case for your business.

It works on every channel

A single shoot produces your homepage promo, your Google Business Profile cover video, your social reels, your Google Ads creative and your recruitment content. It's the highest leverage creative investment a business can make.

It differentiates you immediately

Most small businesses have no video. The bar is low. A customer comparing two businesses in the same industry — one has a well-made promo, one has a phone photo — will trust the one with the video every time, even if the underlying quality of work is equal.

Questions

The things people ask before they call.

How is promotional video production priced in Melbourne?

At Beets & Co, promotional video projects are fixed-price and scoped to what you need — a single promo video, or a package with multiple cuts (social, website, landscape and portrait). You'll know exactly what's included and the price before anything is booked.

How long does a promotional video take to produce?

Most promotional video projects move from briefing to delivery in 2–4 weeks. This includes a pre-production planning session, a half or full day shoot in Melbourne, editing, colour grading and two rounds of revisions. Rush timelines can be accommodated depending on availability.

What types of businesses do you make promotional videos for?

Beets & Co primarily works with trade businesses, operators, manufacturers and local Melbourne businesses. This includes promotional videos for construction companies, service businesses, equipment dealers and professional services. If you sell something real and want a video that shows it clearly, we can help.

What's the difference between a promotional video and a commercial?

A promotional video is typically 60–180 seconds and lives on your website, Google Business Profile, or social media. It introduces your business, shows your work and gives a potential customer a reason to call. A commercial is usually shorter, made for broadcast or paid media, and built around a single message or offer. We produce both.
